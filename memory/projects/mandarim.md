## Learning Architecture

### 1. Anki (Spaced Repetition)
**Daily: 20-30 min**

- Deck: HSK 1-3 vocabulary + hanzi (characters)
- New cards: 20/day
- Review: ~200-300 cards/day (as progress)
- Settings: Standard (25 ease factor)
- Frequency: Every morning (habits)

**Decks to create:**
- [ ] HSK 1 (150 chars)
- [ ] HSK 2 (300 chars)
- [ ] HSK 3 (600 chars)
- [ ] Audio pronunciation (SRS audio)

### 2. Italki (Active Speaking)
**2x/week: 30 min lessons ($8-15/lesson)**

- Find tutor: Native speaker (Beijing preferred)
- Focus: Conversational (not grammar-heavy)
- Lesson structure:
  - 10 min: Review last week
  - 10 min: New topic (HSK level appropriate)
  - 10 min: Free conversation
- Goal: Speak without fear

**Timeline:**
- [ ] Week 1: Italki profile + find tutor
- [ ] Week 2: First lesson (nervousness is normal)
- [ ] Ongoing: 2x/week consistent

### 3. Input Media (Listening)
**Daily: 15-30 min**

- Podcasts: ChinesePod, RichMandarin, EasyChinese
- YouTube: Slow Chinese, Chinese with Paul
- Streaming: Netflix with Chinese audio + Chinese subs
- Goal: Passively absorb rhythm, tones, common phrases

**Progression:**
- May: Super slow, beginner content
- Jun-Jul: Medium pace, HSK 1-2 level
- Aug-Sep: Regular speed, mixed content
- Oct-Nov: Movies, TV shows (50% comprehension target)

### 4. Reading
**3x/week: 15 min**

- Graded readers (HSK level appropriate)
- Simplified Chinese news (News in Levels)
- Notion daily vocabulary
- Journal prompts in Chinese (5-10 sentences)

## Motivation & Psychology

### Why This Matters
- **Neuroplasticity:** Language learning = brain growth
- **Opportunity:** Opens doors (clients, travel, thinking differently)
- **Pride:** Speaking another language = achievement
- **Confidence:** Each milestone = wins

### When It Gets Hard
- **Month 2-3:** Plateau (tones, characters feel endless)
  - Action: Celebrate small wins, find fun content
- **Month 4:** Boredom with routine
  - Action: Change tutor, new podcast, movie night
- **Month 5:** Near goal but still struggling
  - Action: Double down, practice with tutor more, speak out loud to yourself

### Community & Accountability
- Join Reddit r/chinalanguage (shared struggle, tips)
- Discord language exchange groups
- Tell casal + friends (public commitment = motivation)
- Share progress on blog/Twitter (cool to admit learning)

## Nicho Profissional (Professional Niche)

### Photography in Mandarin
- **Vocabulary needed:**
  - 相机 (xiàng jī) = camera
  - 镜头 (jìng tou) = lens
  - 照明 (zhào míng) = lighting
  - 设置 (shè zhì) = settings
  - 快门 (kuài mén) = shutter
  - 感光度 (gǎn guāng dù) = ISO

- **Phrases:**
  - "你的设置是什么?" (What are your settings?)
  - "这个镜头很好" (This lens is excellent)
  - "我是摄影师" (I am a photographer)
